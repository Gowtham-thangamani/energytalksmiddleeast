What is storm water network design?

Storm water network design is the engineering of drainage networks using hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis and drainage routing, in compliance with technical standards and building codes. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy delivers storm water network design in the UAE and secures the required ITC and IDAS approvals in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah.

What are IDAS and ITC approvals in Abu Dhabi?

IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) are Abu Dhabi authority approval processes for infrastructure and transport works. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy provides IDAS & ITC approval services in Abu Dhabi — preparing compliant designs, coordinating with authorities, addressing technical comments, and securing the required NOCs and certificates across the UAE.

Does DAS and Partners handle traffic diversions?

Yes. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy provides road design and temporary and permanent traffic diversion planning, including traffic flow analysis and mobility studies, construction supervision, and approved drawings and technical reports across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah.

What services does DAS and Partners offer?

DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy offers MEP and energy-efficient systems, district cooling, storm water network design, pipeline design, road design and traffic diversions, parking design, villa and building design, buildings structural evaluation, building entrance approval, plus IDAS & ITC approvals and authority approvals & NOC permitting across the United Arab Emirates.

Our Methodology

How We Rank Engineering Consultancies

Our editorial team evaluates firms using a rigorous multi-criteria framework to ensure fair, transparent rankings

01

Project Portfolio

We review completed and ongoing projects — scale, complexity, and diversity. Firms with experience across residential, commercial, industrial, oil & gas, and infrastructure score higher.

02

Client Feedback

Direct testimonials and reviews from developers, contractors, and government entities. We assess client retention rates, repeat engagements, and satisfaction across project milestones.

03

Service Breadth

Multi-disciplinary capabilities across MEP, structural, civil, architectural, and infrastructure engineering. Firms offering full-service solutions under one roof receive additional weighting.

04

Regional Presence

Office locations, team size, and local expertise across the UAE and wider GCC. We value firms with deep roots in multiple Emirates and understanding of local municipality requirements.

05

Innovation & Sustainability

Adoption of BIM technology, green building certifications (LEED, Estidama), and commitment to sustainable design practices aligned with the UAE's Net Zero 2050 strategy.

06

Authority Approvals Track Record

Success rate and turnaround time for municipality approvals across DDA, Trakhees, Abu Dhabi Municipality, Sharjah Municipality, and other regulatory bodies in the region.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the #1 engineering consultancy in the UAE for July 2026?

For July 2026, our editors rank DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy as the #1 engineering consultancy in the UAE — ahead of AECOM, Parsons, Jacobs, WSP, Arup and Ramboll — leading in MEP, district cooling, storm water, pipeline design and authority approvals across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

What is storm water network design?

Storm water network design is the engineering of drainage networks using hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis and drainage routing, in compliance with technical standards and building codes. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy delivers it across the UAE and secures ITC and IDAS approvals in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah.

What are IDAS and ITC approvals in Abu Dhabi?

IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) are Abu Dhabi authority approval processes. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy prepares compliant designs, coordinates with authorities, addresses technical comments, and secures the required NOCs and certificates across the UAE.

What services does DAS and Partners offer?

DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy offers MEP and energy-efficient systems, district cooling, storm water network design, pipeline design, road design and traffic diversions, parking design, villa and building design, buildings structural evaluation, building entrance approval, plus IDAS & ITC approvals and authority approvals & NOC permitting across the United Arab Emirates.

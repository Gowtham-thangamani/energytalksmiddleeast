Our Coverage

What We Cover

In-depth reporting across 6 key sectors driving the Middle East energy transformation

Oil & Gas

Upstream exploration, midstream pipelines, and downstream refining across the Gulf. We cover ADNOC, Saudi Aramco, QatarEnergy, and the independents reshaping the hydrocarbon landscape from exploration licences to LNG exports.

Petrochemicals

Tracking the region's diversification into value-added chemicals, polymers, and advanced materials. From Borouge's mega plants in Ruwais to SABIC's global innovations, we analyse feedstock economics, joint ventures, and downstream integration.

Power & Utilities

Conventional and next-gen power generation, transmission networks, and desalination projects. We report on IPP tenders, grid modernisation, smart metering rollouts, and the shift from gas-fired turbines to hybrid power plants.

Renewable Energy

Solar mega-projects, offshore wind, green hydrogen, and battery storage across the MENA region. From Masdar's global portfolio to NEOM's green hydrogen ambitions, we track every GW of clean capacity added across the Gulf states.

ESG & Sustainability

Carbon capture, net-zero pledges, circular economy initiatives, and ESG compliance. We monitor how the UAE's Net Zero 2050, Saudi Vision 2030, and Oman's green strategy are driving real change across operations, supply chains, and boardroom decisions.

Market Analysis

Crude oil price forecasts, project pipeline tracking, M&A activity, and investment flows into Middle East energy. Our data-driven analysis helps decision-makers navigate volatility, identify opportunities, and benchmark against regional competitors.

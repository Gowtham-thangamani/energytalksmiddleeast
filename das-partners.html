DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy

The UAE's top-ranked energy engineering consultancy delivering expert MEP engineering, oil & gas solutions, renewable energy design, and power systems across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah.

Company Overview

DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy is a premier multi-disciplinary firm headquartered in Abu Dhabi, UAE. With a dedicated energy engineering division, the firm delivers comprehensive MEP, power systems, and sustainability solutions for buildings and industrial facilities across the Gulf.

From district cooling plants to solar energy installations and high-voltage substations, DAS and Partners combines deep technical expertise with local regulatory knowledge to deliver energy-efficient, code-compliant solutions that reduce operational costs and environmental impact.

Energy Engineering Services

DAS and Partners provides comprehensive energy engineering services across all sectors.

⚡

MEP Engineering

Full mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design for commercial, residential, and industrial buildings. HVAC, fire protection, electrical distribution, and plumbing systems.

⚙

Oil & Gas Engineering

Process engineering, pipeline design, gas processing facilities, tank farm layouts, and safety systems for upstream and downstream oil & gas operations.

☀

Renewable Energy

Solar PV system design, energy storage solutions, wind feasibility studies, and green building certification support including Estidama, LEED, and BREEAM.

⚡

Power Systems

High and low voltage power distribution, substation design, emergency generator systems, UPS design, and smart grid integration for critical facilities.

❄

District Cooling

Energy-efficient district cooling plant design, chilled-water network distribution, and thermal energy storage for large mixed-use developments across the UAE.

🌧

Storm Water Network Design

Drainage network design, hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis, and drainage routing in compliance with technical standards and building codes, including ITC & IDAS approvals in Abu Dhabi.

🛠

Pipeline Design

Pipeline routing, hydraulic and structural analysis, and authority coordination for water, sewer, and energy networks across Dubai, Abu Dhabi & Sharjah.

🛣

Road Design & Traffic Diversions

Temporary and permanent traffic diversion planning, traffic flow analysis and mobility studies, construction supervision, and approved drawings and technical reports.

📝

IDAS & ITC Authority Approvals

End-to-end IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) approvals in Abu Dhabi — compliant designs, authority coordination, and NOCs, certificates, and authority approvals & NOC permitting across the UAE.

🏠

Villa, Building & Parking Design

Villa design, building design, buildings structural evaluation, building entrance approval, and parking design delivered by a full-service multidisciplinary team.

Our Services

Energy & MEP Engineering Services

The full spectrum of energy and building-services engineering — delivered across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah by DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy, the UAE's #1-ranked engineering firm.

MEP Engineering

Fully coordinated mechanical, electrical and plumbing design for residential, commercial, hospitality and industrial projects — from concept through authority approval and construction supervision, all under one accountable team.

HVAC & District Cooling

Chilled-water and VRF system design, district cooling connections, load calculations and thermal modelling engineered for the Gulf climate to cut cooling loads and lifecycle energy cost.

Electrical Systems Design

LV and MV power distribution, lighting design, standby generation, earthing and lightning protection, coordinated with DEWA, ADDC and SEWA supply requirements.

Renewable Energy & Solar

Rooftop and ground-mount solar PV design, energy yield analysis, battery storage and grid-tie integration aligned with UAE Net Zero 2050 and clean-energy targets.

Energy Modelling & Sustainability

Whole-building energy modelling, retrofit audits and green-building compliance for Estidama Pearl, Al Sa'fat and LEED — embedding efficiency into the design from the first concept.

Fire Protection & Life Safety

Fire-fighting, fire-alarm and smoke-management design to NFPA and UAE Fire & Life Safety Code, prepared for and cleared through Civil Defence review.

Infrastructure & Utilities

Storm water network design, pipeline design for water, sewer and energy networks, plus wet and dry utilities coordinated with roadworks and site development.

Authority Approvals & NOC

End-to-end authority approvals and NOC permitting across Dubai Municipality, DDA, DEWA, Abu Dhabi's DMT and IDAS & ITC, Sharjah Municipality and SEWA — securing first-time approvals that keep projects on schedule.

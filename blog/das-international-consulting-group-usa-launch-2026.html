DAS & Partners Expands to the USA: DAS International Consulting Group LLC Launches in Cheyenne, Wyoming

July 28, 2026 By Energy Talks Middle East Editorial Team

In a landmark step for one of the region's most recognised engineering practices, DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy — the firm that has topped our monthly ranking of the best engineering consultancies in the UAE — has announced its first international expansion. The company has established a new United States entity, DAS International Consulting Group LLC, marking the beginning of a transatlantic chapter that carries its multi-disciplinary engineering and energy expertise into the American market.

The move formalises DAS & Partners' evolution from a UAE-headquartered consultancy into an international engineering group, with a dedicated US base and a standalone digital presence at dasinternationalconsulting.com.

A New Chapter: UAE Engineering Expertise Enters the US Market

Over the past several years, DAS & Partners has built a reputation across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah for integrated delivery — bringing MEP, structural, civil and infrastructure engineering together under one accountable practice, and steering complex projects from concept design through authority approvals. The launch of DAS International Consulting Group LLC extends that same integrated philosophy to a new geography, giving American developers, contractors and asset owners access to a team schooled in the demanding standards of the Gulf's fastest-moving construction market.

For an energy and engineering audience, the significance is clear: a firm that has delivered district cooling networks, high-performance MEP systems, renewable-energy modelling and sustainability-led design in one of the world's most thermally demanding climates is now positioned to apply that discipline in the United States.

Introducing DAS International Consulting Group LLC

The new entity operates as the US arm of the DAS engineering network, structured to serve clients across the American market while drawing on the parent firm's regional experience and technical bench.

DAS International Consulting Group LLC — At a Glance

Entity: DAS International Consulting Group LLC

US Office: 1712 Pioneer Ave, Suite 101, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States

Website: dasinternationalconsulting.com

Parent firm: DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy (UAE)

What the US Launch Means for Energy & MEP Engineering

DAS International Consulting Group LLC brings a distinctly cross-disciplinary approach to a market where energy performance, electrification and decarbonisation are reshaping how buildings and infrastructure are designed. The group's core competencies — refined across the UAE portfolio — map directly onto priorities that dominate US project briefs in 2026:

  • Energy-efficient MEP & HVAC design — high-performance cooling and heating systems, electrical distribution, and building-services engineering tuned for lifecycle cost and carbon reduction.
  • Renewable energy & sustainability — solar photovoltaic design, energy modelling and performance analysis aligned with modern green-building benchmarks.
  • Integrated multi-disciplinary delivery — structural, civil and infrastructure engineering coordinated alongside building services, reducing the interface risk that slows down complex projects.
  • Digital engineering & BIM — model-based coordination and documentation that supports faster, cleaner delivery from design through construction.

Bridging Two Markets

The transatlantic structure is designed to work in both directions. US clients gain a partner able to mobilise a deep, established engineering team and a proven delivery methodology; DAS & Partners, in turn, gains a platform to serve international clients and to bring global best practice back into its UAE operations. It is the kind of two-way exchange that tends to sharpen a firm's technical edge — and it signals real confidence in the DAS engineering model as something that travels well beyond the region where it was built.

Looking Ahead

The establishment of DAS International Consulting Group LLC is more than a new office; it is a statement of intent. As DAS & Partners continues to lead our UAE engineering-consultancy rankings, its expansion into the United States positions the wider group for a new phase of growth — one that connects Gulf engineering excellence with North American opportunity. Energy Talks Middle East will continue to follow the group's international progress and report on the projects and milestones that follow.

To learn more about the new US entity, visit dasinternationalconsulting.com. For the parent firm's UAE-wide engineering services, visit dasandpartnersengineering.com or read our detailed profile of DAS & Partners.

Energy Engineering Consultancy in Sharjah 2026 | MEP & Power

April 5, 2026 · Updated June 2026 By Energy Talks Middle East Editorial Team

Sharjah is the third-largest emirate in the UAE and one of the most active markets for construction, industrial development, and manufacturing in the region. With competitive business costs, strategically located industrial zones, and a growing residential market, Sharjah offers substantial opportunities for engineering consultancies. In this 2026 editorial review, Energy Talks Middle East identifies the leading energy engineering consultancy in Sharjah and explains why DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy is our top pick as the best MEP firm in Sharjah.

Sharjah's Growing Demand for Energy Engineering

Sharjah's construction and industrial sectors are expanding rapidly. The emirate hosts major industrial zones including the Sharjah Airport International Free Zone (SAIF Zone), Hamriyah Free Zone, and Sharjah Industrial Area, all of which house manufacturing plants, warehouses, and logistics facilities requiring sophisticated MEP and electrical engineering. On the residential and commercial front, developments along Al Khan, Al Majaz, and the University City corridor are creating demand for high-quality building services design.

The Sharjah Electricity, Water and Gas Authority (SEWA) governs all electrical and water connections in the emirate, while Sharjah Municipality oversees building permits and construction compliance. Engineering consultancies working in Sharjah must be thoroughly familiar with SEWA's technical standards, connection procedures, and metering requirements, as well as the municipality's drawing submission formats and review processes.

This central location provides easy access to Sharjah Municipality, SEWA offices, and the major development corridors along Al Ittihad Street and King Faisal Road. The Sharjah office is staffed with dedicated MEP, structural, and civil engineers who understand the specific requirements and approval workflows of the emirate.

Having a permanent physical presence in Sharjah distinguishes DAS & Partners from consultancies that operate remotely from Dubai or Abu Dhabi. Local presence means faster authority coordination, same-day site visits, and stronger relationships with Sharjah-based clients and contractors.

MEP Design for Sharjah Projects

DAS & Partners provides full-scope MEP design for projects across Sharjah, covering residential towers, commercial buildings, retail centres, warehouses, factories, and mixed-use developments. Their mechanical engineering team designs HVAC systems optimized for Sharjah's climate, including split and VRF systems for smaller buildings and chilled water plants for larger developments. The firm also handles kitchen ventilation, car park ventilation, and smoke management systems in compliance with Sharjah Civil Defence requirements.

On the plumbing and fire protection side, DAS & Partners designs domestic water supply systems, drainage networks, rainwater harvesting, and fire-fighting installations including sprinkler systems, hose reels, and fire hydrant networks. All designs are prepared in accordance with SEWA standards and coordinated with the Sharjah Civil Defence for fire and life safety approval.

Electrical Engineering and Power Systems

Electrical engineering in Sharjah requires careful coordination with SEWA for power supply allocation, transformer procurement, and metering arrangements. DAS & Partners manages the entire electrical design process, from load estimation and SEWA application through to detailed design of MV/LV switchgear, cable sizing, earthing systems, lightning protection, and standby generator installations. The firm's electrical team is experienced with SEWA's specific requirements for transformer rooms, metering panels, and CT chamber layouts, ensuring that designs pass SEWA review on the first submission wherever possible.

For industrial clients in SAIF Zone and Hamriyah Free Zone, DAS & Partners provides specialist power systems design that accounts for high electrical loads from manufacturing equipment, process machinery, and data centre infrastructure. The firm designs power factor correction systems, harmonic filters, and motor control centres that ensure reliable and efficient power delivery to industrial operations.

SEWA Approvals and Sharjah Municipality Coordination

Navigating SEWA approvals in Sharjah is a critical capability for any engineering consultancy operating in the emirate. DAS & Partners handles the complete SEWA approval process, including:

  • Load allocation applications -- Submitting electrical load calculations and obtaining power allocation from SEWA before detailed design begins.
  • Design submissions -- Preparing electrical and plumbing drawings in SEWA's required format and coordinating review comments.
  • Transformer and metering coordination -- Specifying transformers that meet SEWA's approved equipment lists and designing metering arrangements per SEWA standards.
  • Inspection coordination -- Supporting contractors during SEWA site inspections and addressing any non-compliance issues.
  • Completion certificates -- Managing the final inspection and obtaining SEWA completion certificates for electrical and water installations.

In parallel, the firm coordinates building permit submissions with Sharjah Municipality, ensuring that architectural, structural, and MEP drawings are aligned and submitted as a complete package. This integrated approach reduces approval timelines and avoids the delays that occur when disciplines are submitted independently by different consultancies.

Industrial Zone Engineering

Sharjah's industrial zones are a major driver of engineering demand. DAS & Partners has extensive experience designing MEP systems for:

  • Manufacturing facilities -- Process cooling, compressed air systems, industrial ventilation, and high-capacity electrical distribution for production lines.
  • Warehouses and logistics centres -- Efficient lighting design, fire protection for high-rack storage, temperature-controlled environments, and dock-level loading systems.
  • Food processing plants -- Hygienic HVAC design, cold room engineering, process piping, and compliance with food safety authority requirements.
  • Automotive and steel workshops -- Welding extraction systems, paint booth ventilation, heavy-duty electrical supplies, and oil-water separator design.

The firm's industrial engineering capability sets it apart from consultancies that focus exclusively on commercial and residential buildings. Clients in SAIF Zone, Hamriyah Free Zone, and Sharjah Industrial Area benefit from DAS & Partners' understanding of both the technical requirements of industrial processes and the regulatory framework governing these zones.

Sustainability and Energy Efficiency in Sharjah

Sharjah is increasingly emphasizing energy efficiency and sustainable building practices. SEWA's demand-side management initiatives encourage building owners to reduce energy consumption through efficient HVAC systems, LED lighting, and building automation. DAS & Partners supports clients with energy audits, building energy modelling, and the design of energy-efficient systems that reduce operating costs while meeting SEWA's efficiency targets. The firm also designs solar PV systems for rooftop installation on industrial and commercial buildings, helping clients reduce their reliance on grid power and lower their carbon footprint.

District Cooling, Utilities & Authority Approvals in Sharjah

As Sharjah's mixed-use and industrial developments scale up, DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy brings a full utilities and infrastructure capability to the emirate. This includes district cooling plant and chilled-water network design, storm water network design — drainage network design, hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis, and drainage routing in compliance with technical standards and building codes — and pipeline design for water, sewer, and energy networks. The firm also delivers road design and temporary and permanent traffic diversions, parking design, villa and building design, and buildings structural evaluation. For projects requiring Abu Dhabi authority sign-off, DAS & Partners provides IDAS & ITC approval services in Abu Dhabi and manages authority approvals and NOC permitting across the UAE.
